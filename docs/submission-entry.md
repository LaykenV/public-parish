## Description to paste

I built Public Parish because following Louisiana's major projects meant
searching scattered government documents and trying to work out what had
actually been decided.

Public Parish is a free, open-source, nonpartisan civic app. Open a story,
inspect the official passage behind a claim, ask a question and follow the
subject. Accepted evidence changes can produce sourced email updates. Residents
can reply to those emails with questions through the same grounded Ask path.
Reading and Ask need no account.

Every published factual claim links to a retained source snapshot and a precise
citation. Extraction, independent model review and deterministic checks stand
between a retrieved document and publication. Missing evidence stays visible as
a limitation or prevents publication. An agenda does not establish a vote;
a public authorization does not establish completed spending.

The launch includes Meta in Richland Parish, SpaceX in Vermilion Parish and the
Boyce data center, plus ten November 3 constitutional amendment explanations.
Local records cover named bodies in Lafayette, Rapides and East Baton Rouge,
with bounded Louisiana Public Service Commission coverage and a separate Beaver
Lake story. Coverage pages disclose delayed source checks. The ballot guide
quotes official wording and Acts without recommending a vote.

| Sponsor | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Convex | Database, realtime queries, source files, immutable publication versions, durable workflows, scheduled monitoring, Agent threads, rate limiting, Google authentication and static hosting |
| Firecrawl | Discovers and retrieves official records, renders pages, extracts PDFs and checks sources for changes |
| OpenAI | Extracts evidence and drafts stories, reviews with a separate model, and answers questions through Convex AI Gateway |
| AgentMail | Verifies subscriptions, delivers sourced updates and routes inbound questions through the evidence checks |

Try it signed out. Open the [Meta story](https://befitting-flamingo-587.convex.site/stories/meta-richland),
tap a citation and its saved source, then ask, "What must Entergy do with an
early termination fee?" Open the answer's citation. Follow by email if you want
updates. The [ballot guide](https://befitting-flamingo-587.convex.site/ballot)
offers another way to inspect the evidence.

The [2:48 demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zuOhc5rGgsQ) follows this loop
through a clearly labeled founder-test email exchange. The
[complete build log](https://github.com/LaykenV/public-parish/blob/main/hackathon.md)
records development, owner reviews, failed approaches, source corrections and
release checks. Public Parish launched September 18 with posts on X, LinkedIn,
personal Facebook and five Facebook groups. It is also live at
[publicparish.com](https://www.publicparish.com).
