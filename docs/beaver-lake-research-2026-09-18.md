# Beaver Lake source research, September 18, 2026

Beaver Lake belongs in Rapides Parish coverage. The site, local tax exemption
decision and port infrastructure decisions concern Rapides Parish. State agencies
also made decisions, but that does not make this a statewide featured story.
This research supports a separately registered Rapides story without changing the
three featured launch stories.

The strongest retained evidence documents the former SunGas proposal, government
incentive and infrastructure actions, FastSites selection, and LDEQ catalog entries
for June 2026 permit application withdrawals. The official records retained here
do not establish a replacement tenant or the reported land option's terms. The story is published at
[Public Parish](https://www.publicparish.com/stories/beaver-lake-rapides).
This document records its source inventory, limits and release checks.

## How to describe the money

The amounts describe different decisions and stages. Do not add them into a
single public spending total.

- $1.8 billion was the private project's proposed investment in the 2023 LED
  announcement. The jobs figures were conditional projections.
- $6 million was a prepared performance-based infrastructure grant in that same
  announcement. The record does not show a payment or that the conditions were met.
- $100,000 was the maximum authorized port surveying and engineering task order.
  An authorization is not an invoice or proof of payment.
- $3.75 million was the RRWC funding request approved in October 2025 and the
  agreement and fund reservation approved in November 2025. Count this as one
  authorization sequence. The minutes do not show disbursement, cancellation or
  reassignment after the June 2026 application-withdrawal records.
- $15 million was conditional port financing described in a June 2025 fiscal
  note. It was not an established debt issuance. The note's separate $10 million
  approval belonged to the electrical substation project.
- FastSites selection did not establish a site-specific award amount in the
  retained announcement.

## LDEQ identity and document limits

The official public notice register supplies the facility name that connects
AI 248712 with Beaver Lake Renewable Energy. The four EDMS viewers supply document
metadata for that same agency interest number. These two types of records support
a narrow statement that LDEQ lists application-withdrawal documents for Beaver
Lake in June 2026.

Do not describe the entries as revocation of issued permits, proof that every
permit was withdrawn, or a complete account of the project's legal status. The
register's "Issue Date" column also does not establish permit issuance without
the underlying decision. The saved viewers do not include the withdrawal
letters' text, signatures or stated reasons. The full air and water permit files,
draft permits, technical analyses and application attachments were excluded from
this bounded processing run. They may answer later questions, but their contents
cannot be asserted from these snapshots.

Revolution Aluminum Propco's landfill record, AI 195565, is a distinct facility
record. A shared industrial-site history does not allow its permits or regulatory
actions to be attributed to Beaver Lake's AI 248712.
