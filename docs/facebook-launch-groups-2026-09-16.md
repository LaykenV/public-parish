# Facebook launch groups and posting strategy

Research date: September 16, 2026. Counts and rules are observations from that
date and should be checked again before posting.

This report screens 75 groups. It identifies 46 launch candidates, including four
that need a posting-format or administrator-fit check. Another 15 require an
exception or prior participation. The remaining 14 are poor fits, ineligible, or
in another state. Personal membership history and application answers are kept
outside this public document.

The founder completed design and QA on September 16. Use [work](work.md) for
current launch status and the LPSC source follow-up, and [marketing](marketing.md)
for positioning and publication requirements. This research does not establish
posting permission, resident use, or conversion results. It does not authorize
posts or administrator outreach.

## Recommended launch sequence

Use video selectively and post in small batches. Start with a local decision and
one specific claim supported by its document. Identify the builder relationship
and show what the source establishes and what remains unresolved.

1. Start with a personal Facebook post and a 30- to 45-second recording. Show one
   story, open its official source, and demonstrate one useful question. Include
   captions and the relevant direct link. Check the actual preview before posting.
2. Make short Boyce, SpaceX and Meta versions from the same recording. Each clip
   should answer one reader question without requiring the general launch video.
   Statewide amendment explanations are another option where group rules allow
   them. Use the official wording and Act without recommending a vote.
3. Follow with four permitted group posts matched to their audiences. The first
   batch below is a proposed test, not a claim about the best posting time.
4. Spend the next 48 hours answering readers and fixing confusing explanations.
   Compare story visits, source opens, useful questions and follows. Try a short
   explanation with a story link in some groups and a relevant clip in others.
   There is no measured evidence yet that video performs better for this app.
5. Aim for 8 to 12 suitable groups in the first week, then expand where responses
   justify it. The full inventory is a research pool, not a day-one posting quota.
   Do not repeat an announcement just because its first post received little
   attention. Follow up when a new source or a useful answer gives readers a
   reason to return.

nonpartisan does not override a no-promotion or no-politics rule. Ask an admin
when permission is unclear. Do not infer endorsement from a group's name or
membership approval. Do not sum member counts as unique reach; audiences overlap.

## Research coverage and limits

The research used Facebook group search, About descriptions, displayed rules,
expanded descriptions, activity counts, related-group suggestions and selected
public discussion feeds. Group links above are the source pages. Descriptions
and rules were summarized; this is not a full moderation audit of every post.

Searches covered Boyce, Rapides, CenLA, Richland, Richland Parish, Rayville, Delhi
Louisiana, Monroe Louisiana, Baton Rouge, Baton Rouge community, Baton Rouge
news, Vermilion, Acadiana, Pecan Island, Abbeville, Lafayette Louisiana,
Alexandria Louisiana community, Pineville Louisiana, Carencro, Broussard,
Scott Louisiana, Youngsville Louisiana, Duson, Louisiana entrepreneurs,
Meta Richland, SpaceX Louisiana, Louisiana data center, and Northeast Louisiana
community. Broader searches often returned already-reviewed groups, resale
feeds, jobs or housing groups, narrow membership groups, or wrong-state matches.

Facebook search is personalized, ranked and incomplete. This is a broad research
pass, not proof that every relevant group has been found. Hidden groups, changed
rules and future groups require fresh checks. Public Parish's supported coverage
must be described according to [sources](sources.md), regardless of where a
Facebook audience lives.
